The Computer Group

The syndicate that bet by computer

In the late 1970s and early 1980s a small group of bettors started using computer models to price college and professional games. When their number and the bookmakers' line disagreed, they bet, and they bet big.

By the mid-1980s the group was moving millions of dollars a year through Las Vegas sportsbooks. The operation ended after FBI raids in January 1985, and Sports Illustrated brought the story to a national audience in March 1986.

Computer Prediction is built on the same idea: let the numbers decide, bet only when a pick passes the test, and keep strict, flat stakes.

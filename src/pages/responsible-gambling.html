Responsible Gambling

Betting should stay entertainment. Set a budget you can afford to lose, size bets in small units, and never chase losses.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned, or more to win back losses
  • Borrowing money or hiding bets from people close to you
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when you are not betting

Get help

  • 1-800-GAMBLER (US), free and confidential, 24/7
  • National Council on Problem Gambling: ncpgambling.org
  • Most sportsbooks offer deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion in account settings
